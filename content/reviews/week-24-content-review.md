# Week 24 content and lab review

## Scope and disposition

Roadmap source: `Path.md`, Week 24 — Project storytelling and mock interviews.

The reviewed scope adds two lesson depths, strict evidence contracts, a deterministic
three-depth story builder, a six-dimension practice rubric, CLI, tests and a
100-item assessment. Publication was approved on 2026-11-12.

## Coverage

| Requirement | Candidate treatment |
| --- | --- |
| Project deep dives | situation/task/role/actions/decisions/metrics/failure/limit/reflection |
| Multiple durations | 2-minute, 10-minute and deep-dive packs from one record |
| Design defence | reason, rejected alternative and consequence per decision |
| Evaluation/security/cost/failure | follow-up prompts and evidence-source discipline |
| Leadership | situation, action, observable result and learning |
| Honest impact | metric provenance plus individual/team/organization attribution |

## Review findings

The transformation never changes a metric's declared attribution. The short
story uses one action and metric; the ten-minute pack adds decision, failure,
leadership and reflection; the deep dive adds remaining evidence. The fictional
fixture is repeatedly labelled synthetic and must not be reused as personal
experience.

The rubric checks presence and specificity, not external truth or communication
quality. Confidential-data review and employer permission are separate human
gates. Ruff, formatting, strict mypy, focused behavior/CLI tests and the bank
contracts pass locally. Completed live checks are recorded in the
[Weeks 21–30 publication verification](weeks-21-30-publication-verification.md).
