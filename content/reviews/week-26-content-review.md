# Week 26 content and lab review

## Scope and disposition

Roadmap source: `Path.md`, Week 26 — Data preparation. The reviewed lesson,

## Coverage and findings

The two lesson depths make permission, provenance, versioned exclusions,
deduplication, benchmark contamination, privacy, source mixing, grouped splits,
tokenisation, packing and manifests explicit. The deterministic lab implements
allowlist gates, exact hashes, source caps, basic redaction, document splitting,
training-only vocabulary and fixed sequences.

Tests cover rejection reasons, PII examples, contamination, disjoint partitions,
reproducibility, config hashes, strict inputs and CLI behavior. The content
states that regex redaction, exact hashes and automated rights fields do not
replace privacy, semantic-deduplication or legal review.
