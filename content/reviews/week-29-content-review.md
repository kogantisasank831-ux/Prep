# Week 29 content and lab review

## Scope and disposition

Roadmap source: `Path.md`, Week 29 — Model evaluation. The candidate adds two
lesson depths and a deterministic four-candidate comparison. It remains
unpublished pending human approval.

## Coverage and findings

The lessons separate perplexity, exact match, classification, segmented,
operational and preference evidence. They require identical frozen cases,
contamination disclosure, paired uncertainty, blinded human review and
validation of any LLM judge against people.

The lab enforces a complete candidate-by-example matrix, calculates metric
arithmetic and deterministic paired bootstrap intervals, and keeps pairwise
votes separate. Tests cover normalisation, contamination scope, expected
candidate ordering, precision/recall/F1, perplexity, comparisons, votes,
malformed matrices, strict inputs and CLI behavior. All fixtures are explicitly
synthetic and cannot establish real model quality.
