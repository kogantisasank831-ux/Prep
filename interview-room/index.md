## How this companion fits Prep

Prep remains the reviewed source for the 30-week learning path, role-focused interview banks, and 40 revision cheat sheets. [Interview Room](https://deepankarkotnala.github.io/interview_prep/) adds an external practice environment for active recall, timed speaking, round-specific preparation, and visual system explanations.

Use the two resources together:

1. Learn or refresh a topic in the Prep roadmap and cheat sheets.
2. Answer related Interview Room questions aloud before revealing guidance.
3. Return to Prep and mark the relevant cheat sheet revised only when you can explain the mechanism, trade-offs, failure modes, and a production example from memory.

> **Source boundary:** Interview Room is an external companion maintained in [`deepankarkotnala/interview_prep`](https://github.com/deepankarkotnala/interview_prep) and reviewed here at source commit `b487032a09e7ae3e0630cf1da660b62a3d988b4a`. Prep links to its material but does not copy or certify its answers. Recheck time-sensitive model, pricing, framework, regulation, and standards claims before relying on them.

## Choose a practice mode

| Mode | Best use |
| --- | --- |
| [Today’s review](https://deepankarkotnala.github.io/interview_prep/review.html) | Work through due and new questions, reveal the guidance, then rate recall. |
| [Mock round](https://deepankarkotnala.github.io/interview_prep/mock.html) | Rehearse a timed interview sequence with follow-up pressure. |
| [Rehearsal room](https://deepankarkotnala.github.io/interview_prep/rehearsal.html) | Draw one question, start the timer, and practise speaking without notes. |
| [Browse by interview round](https://deepankarkotnala.github.io/interview_prep/rounds.html) | Prepare separately for screening, technical, system-design, manager, and HR rounds. |
| [Browse by employer type](https://deepankarkotnala.github.io/interview_prep/tracks.html) | Adjust the same core answer for product, consulting, banking, healthcare, retail, or India-focused companies. |
| [Visual explanations](https://deepankarkotnala.github.io/interview_prep/visuals.html) | Walk through transformers, inference, LoRA, RAG, agents, MCP, LangGraph, guardrails, and evaluation visually. |

## Recommended weekly rhythm

- **During the week:** learn from the roadmap and revise the matching cheat sheet.
- **Twice a week:** use rehearsal mode for five short spoken answers.
- **At the weekend:** complete one timed mock and note weak topics.
- **Before an interview:** filter by the expected round and employer type, then practise the weakest topic banks first.
