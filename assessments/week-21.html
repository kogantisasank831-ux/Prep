Your paper

Each paper draws one foundation and one applied question from each format—single-choice, multiple-correct, matching, short syntax and Python coding. All ten questions are worth 10 marks each.

This is a learning check, not a pricing calculator. Practise comparing identical workloads, routing with a quality floor, budgeting required and optional context, separating TTFT from completion latency, limiting parallel work and scoping caches. All economic and performance values in the lesson lab are synthetic.

Scoring, coding and saved progress
  • Single-choice and short answers score 10 or 0. Multiple-correct requires the complete set. Matching awards 2.5 per pair.
  • Coding awards two marks per passed check. Python runs locally in a disposable browser worker with a five-second execution limit after loading.
  • Progress stays in this browser. Public answer keys and downloaded results are practice evidence, not authenticated exam records.
