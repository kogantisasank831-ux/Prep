Your paper

Each paper draws one foundation and one applied question from each format—single-choice, multiple-correct, matching, short syntax and Python coding. All ten questions are worth 10 marks each.

Practise permission-first ingestion, traceable rejection reasons, cross-split deduplication, contamination and privacy controls, source balance, group splitting and deterministic output manifests.

Scoring, coding and saved progress
  • Single-choice and short answers score 10 or 0. Multiple-correct requires the complete set. Matching awards 2.5 per pair.
  • Coding awards two marks per passed check and runs locally in a disposable browser worker.
  • Public answer keys and downloaded results are practice evidence, not authenticated exam records.
